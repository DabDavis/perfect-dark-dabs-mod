# French glossary (lang/fr)

Our own translation, made from the English. Rare's PAL French was consulted
only for proper-noun conventions (level names, a few established names); no
sentence of theirs is used. Keep these terms when translating the port's
strings (`port.json`) and GE Plus (`ge.json`).

## Register

- The game speaks to the player with **vous** (menus, help, training, HUD).
- **Carrington <-> Joanna: vous** both ways (he is her employer, formal and
  paternal: "Joanna, vous devez...").
- **Joanna <-> Elvis, Jonathan, Velvet, Grimshaw, Foster, CI staff: tu.**
- Enemies/guards talking among themselves: tu. Villains to Joanna: Cassandra
  and Trent use **vous** (cold, haughty); guards, Mr. Blonde's thugs and the
  Skedar use **tu**.
- The President and Joanna: vous.

## Typography

- « guillemets » with a no-break space inside; no-break space (U+00A0) before
  `: ; ! ?` (the build helper converts a plain space there automatically).
- Capitals keep their accents (É, À, Ç): the font composes them.
- `'` ASCII apostrophe; `...` three dots (not the ellipsis character).
- Strings the English writes in ALL CAPS stay ALL CAPS.
- Numbers: 1 000 not used (the game has none); time 4 secondes, "s" in HUD.

## Names kept as they are

## Pickup messages (propobj)

Perfect Dark builds "Picked up " + "a " + name + "." from pieces and a pack
cannot leave a piece empty, so French uses "Obtenu" + " : " (the determiner
pieces a/an/some/the) + name: "Obtenu : grenade." In split screen the
determiner pieces "A "/"Some " become "+1 "/"+ ": "+1 grenade." The plural "s"
is appended by the game, so ammo names end on the noun that takes it
(cartouche, balle, grenade, roquette, missile, trait, fléchette); "knife" is
built "couteau" + " de combat" / "x de combat".

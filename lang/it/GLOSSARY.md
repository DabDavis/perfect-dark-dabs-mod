# Italian glossary (lang/it)

Our own translation, written from the English alone: no text of Rare's (PAL
or JPN) and no other pack's translation was read or used. Keep these terms
when translating anything else for the pack (`pd/`, `port.json`, `ge.json`).

## Register

- **The game speaks to the player with tu** (menus, help, training, HUD,
  objectives): "Premi il pulsante Z", "Seleziona", "Hai raccolto...".
- **Objective list lines are infinitives** ("Disattivare il nodo di
  sicurezza"); **HUD orders are tu imperatives** ("Recupera la collana.").
- **Carrington <-> Joanna: tu** both ways (a close, paternal employer; he
  calls her Joanna, she calls him Daniel or Carrington).
- **Joanna <-> Elvis, Jonathan, Velvet, Grimshaw, Foster, the Institute's
  staff: tu.**
- **The President: Lei** both ways (he to Joanna and she to him); Trent and
  the NSA men speak to the President with Lei.
- **Dr. Caroll (the AI) <-> Joanna: Lei** (a courteous, fussy scientist).
- **Cassandra De Vries to Joanna: Lei** (cold and haughty); Trent, Mr. Blonde,
  the guards and the Skedar speak to her with **tu**. Guards among
  themselves: tu. Addressing a group: voi.
- **GoldenEye: M, Q and the Defence Minister <-> Bond: Lei.** Moneypenny,
  Trevelyan, Natalya (after they meet), Valentin, Boris, Xenia <-> Bond: tu.
  GoldenEye's briefings speak to 007 with Lei; its objectives are
  infinitives.

## Typography

- Quotes: « » (no spaces inside); `'` is the ASCII apostrophe; `...` three
  dots, not the ellipsis character.
- Accents: à è é ì ò ù as Italian spells them (perché, però, città, è, È).
  A capital E with an accent is **È**, never E' (the font composes it).
- Strings the English writes in ALL CAPS stay ALL CAPS, accents included
  ("MISSIONE COMPIUTA", "PERCHÉ").
- Times and dates: "02:30  15 AGO 2023" style (month names abbreviated
  GEN FEB MAR APR MAG GIU LUG AGO SET OTT NOV DIC).
- Every control code exactly as the English: `\n` (and the trailing one),
  `|` section markers, `%s`/`%d` in the same order and number, `\0` (the
  NUL separator in the hangar/location bios), leading/trailing spaces.

## Pickup messages (propobj)

Perfect Dark builds "Picked up " + determiner + name + plural "s" + "." from
pieces, and a pack cannot leave a piece empty (an empty value means "not
translated" and the English comes back). Italian cannot form a plural by
adding a letter, so:

- "Picked up " -> "Raccolto"; the determiners in full screen ("a ", "an ",
  "some ", "the ", "your ") -> ": " so the message is "Raccolto: granata.";
- in split screen, where there is no "Picked up", "A "/"An " -> "1 ",
  "Some "/"The "/"Your " -> "+ ";
- the names are the singular noun, and the plural piece "s" is " (+)", so
  more than one reads "Raccolto: granata (+).";
- "combat " + "knife"/"knives" become "coltell" + "o tattico"/"i tattici".

## Lengths

- Menu labels do not wrap: stay within about 1.3x the English. Drop articles
  and use short words before abbreviating ("Impost. partita" only if needed).
- `--lang-audit` lists about 660 strings over 1.3x the English; nearly all are
  dialogue, HUD messages and objectives (which wrap), or short words beside
  shorter English ("Back" -> "Indietro") that fit their boxes. Labels that were
  long in a real menu were shortened ("Tieni", "Preset telecamera",
  "- Neutralizzazione", "Domande sicurezza: ...").
- Inventory names stay at 19 characters or fewer ("Falcon 2 (silenz.)",
  "Fucile da cecchino", "Coltello tattico").
- Team names at most 11 bytes (accented letters are two bytes), the default
  player name "Player" at most 10 ("Giocatore").
